# PA204　To what extent do you agree or disagree 

**題組代碼**：`PA204`　**題數**：7　**問卷**：家長問卷  
**Annex B 表**：—

## 題幹



> To what extent do you agree or disagree with the following statements about your child?

## 選項量尺

非常不同意（Strongly disagree） ｜ 不同意（Disagree） ｜ 同意（Agree） ｜ 非常同意（Strongly agree） ｜ valid skip (filter rule)（valid skip (filter rule)） ｜ system missing（system missing） ｜ not administered/not applicable（not administered/not applicable） ｜ invalid（invalid） ｜ no response（no response）

## 題目

**1. My child is curious to learn new things on his/her school subjects.**

　　代碼：`PA204Q01DA`

**2. My child is good at learning new things on his/her own.**

　　代碼：`PA204Q02DA`

**3. My child asks questions whenever he/she does not understand something.**

　　代碼：`PA204Q03DA`

**4. My child is good at managing his/her emotions when learning (e.g., frustration, anxiety, boredom).**

　　代碼：`PA204Q04DA`

**5. My child tries to find solutions to school assignments on his/her own before asking for help.**

　　代碼：`PA204Q07DA`

**6. My child welcomes comments from me on his/her work.**

　　代碼：`PA204Q08DA`

**7. My child is attentive to the feedback he/she receives from teachers.**

　　代碼：`PA204Q09DA`
