# PA201　Think about how your child uses <digital

**題組代碼**：`PA201`　**題數**：5　**問卷**：家長問卷  
**Annex B 表**：—

## 題幹



> Think about how your child uses <digital resources> outside of school. How much do you agree or disagree with the following statements.

## 選項量尺

非常不同意（Strongly disagree） ｜ 不同意（Disagree） ｜ 同意（Agree） ｜ 非常同意（Strongly agree） ｜ Does not apply（Does not apply）

## 題目

**1. My child turns off notifications from social networks and apps on his/her <digital devices> when he/she goes to sleep.**

　　代碼：`PA201Q01DA`

**2. My child feels nervous/anxious when he/she doesn’t have his/her <digital device> nearby.**

　　代碼：`PA201Q02DA`

**3. My child uses <digital resources> for learning activities outside of school.**

　　代碼：`PA201Q03DA`

**4. My child spends too much time playing videogames.**

　　代碼：`PA201Q05DA`

**5. My child spends too much time on social media.**

　　代碼：`PA201Q06DA`
