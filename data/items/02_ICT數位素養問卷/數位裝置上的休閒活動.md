# 數位裝置上的休閒活動

**Annex B 表標題（英文原文）**：Leisure activities on digital devices on a typical weekday  
**題組代碼**：`IC177`　**題數**：14　**問卷**：ICT 數位素養問卷  
**Annex B 表**：Table I.B1.4.37

## 題幹

> 在典型的上學日，你花多少時間做以下休閒活動？

> 原文：During a typical weekday, how much time do you spend doing the following leisure activities?

## 選項量尺

No time at all（No time at all） ｜ Less than 1 hour a day（Less than 1 hour a day） ｜ Between 1 and 3 hours a day（Between 1 and 3 hours a day） ｜ More than 3 hours and up to 5 hours a day（More than 3 hours and up to 5 hours a day） ｜ More than 5 hours and up to 7 hours a day（More than 5 hours and up to 7 hours a day） ｜ More than 7 hours a day（More than 7 hours a day）

## 題目

**1. 玩電玩（用手機、遊戲主機、線上平台或應用程式）**

　　原文：Play video-games (using my smartphone, a gaming console or an online platform or Apps)
　　代碼：`IC177Q01JA`

　　| 選項 | 臺灣 | OECD |
　　|---|--:|--:|
　　| No time at all | 11.19% | 18.9% |
　　| Less than 1 hour a day | 23.12% | 26.25% |
　　| Between 1 and 3 hours a day | 35.41% | 30.36% |
　　| More than 3 hours and up to 5 hours a day | 16.14% | 14.17% |
　　| More than 5 hours and up to 7 hours a day | 6.57% | 5.81% |
　　| More than 7 hours a day | 7.56% | 4.5% |
