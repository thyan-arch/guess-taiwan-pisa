# IC180　To what extent do you agree or disagree 

**題組代碼**：`IC180`　**題數**：6　**問卷**：ICT 數位素養問卷  
**Annex B 表**：—

## 題幹



> To what extent do you agree or disagree with the following statements?

## 選項量尺

非常不同意（Strongly disagree） ｜ 不同意（Disagree） ｜ 同意（Agree） ｜ 非常同意（Strongly agree） ｜ valid skip (filter rule)（valid skip (filter rule)） ｜ system missing（system missing） ｜ not administered/not applicable（not administered/not applicable） ｜ invalid（invalid） ｜ no response（no response）

## 題目

**1. When searching for information online I compare different sources.**

　　代碼：`IC180Q02JA`

**2. I check the accuracy of online information before sharing it on social networks.**

　　代碼：`IC180Q03JA`

**3. I discuss the accuracy of online information with my teachers or in class.**

　　代碼：`IC180Q04JA`

**4. I discuss the accuracy of online information with friends or other students.**

　　代碼：`IC180Q05JA`

**5. I discuss the accuracy of online information with my parents (or family).**

　　代碼：`IC180Q06JA`

**6. I try to flag wrong information when I encounter it online.**

　　代碼：`IC180Q07JA`
