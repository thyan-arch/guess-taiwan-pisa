# 學校運用數位裝置強化教學的能力

**Annex B 表標題（英文原文）**：School's capacity to enhance teaching and learning using digital devices  
**題組代碼**：`IC172`　**題數**：7　**問卷**：ICT 數位素養問卷  
**Annex B 表**：Table I.B1.4.14

## 題幹



> To what extent do you agree or disagree with the following statements?

## 選項量尺

非常不同意（Strongly disagree） ｜ 不同意（Disagree） ｜ 同意（Agree） ｜ 非常同意（Strongly agree） ｜ valid skip (filter rule)（valid skip (filter rule)） ｜ system missing（system missing） ｜ not administered/not applicable（not administered/not applicable） ｜ invalid（invalid） ｜ no response（no response）

## 題目

**1. There are enough <digital resources> for every student at my school.**

　　代碼：`IC172Q01JA`

**2. There are enough digital devices with access to the Internet at my school.**

　　代碼：`IC172Q02JA`

**3. The school’s Internet speed is sufficient.**

　　代碼：`IC172Q03JA`

　　| 選項 | 臺灣 | OECD |
　　|---|--:|--:|
　　| 非常不同意 | 1.95% | 9.21% |
　　| 不同意 | 7.08% | 17.32% |
　　| 同意 | 56.77% | 39.14% |
　　| 非常同意 | 34.2% | 34.33% |

**4. <Digital resources> function properly at my school.**

　　代碼：`IC172Q04JA`

**5. The school provides sufficient technical support to help students in their use of <digital resources>.**

　　代碼：`IC172Q07JA`

**6. Teachers at my school have the necessary skills to use digital devices during instruction.**

　　代碼：`IC172Q08JA`

**7. The school’s Internet is stable.**

　　代碼：`IC172Q10DA`
