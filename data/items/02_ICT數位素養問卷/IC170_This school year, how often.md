## 題幹



> This school year, how often did you use the following <digital resources> at school?

## 選項量尺

從不或幾乎從不（Never or almost never） ｜ 大約一個月一到兩次（About once or twice a month） ｜ 大約一週一到兩次（About once or twice a week） ｜ 每天或幾乎每天（Every day or almost every day） ｜ Several times a day（Several times a day） ｜ This resource is not available to me at school（This resource is not available to me at school）
