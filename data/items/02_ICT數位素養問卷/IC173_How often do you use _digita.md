# IC173　How often do you use <digital resources>

**題組代碼**：`IC173`　**題數**：7　**問卷**：ICT 數位素養問卷  
**Annex B 表**：—

## 題幹

> 在以下科目的課堂上，你有多常用數位資源？

> 原文：How often do you use <digital resources> during the lessons in the following subjects?

## 選項量尺

從不或幾乎從不（Never or almost never） ｜ In less than half of the lessons（In less than half of the lessons） ｜ In about half of the lessons（In about half of the lessons） ｜ In more than half of the lessons（In more than half of the lessons） ｜ In every or almost every lesson（In every or almost every lesson） ｜ I do not have this subject（I do not have this subject）
