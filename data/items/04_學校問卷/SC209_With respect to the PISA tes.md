# SC209　With respect to the PISA test in your sc

**題組代碼**：`SC209`　**題數**：6　**問卷**：學校問卷  
**Annex B 表**：—

## 題幹

> 關於貴校的 PISA 測驗，您是否做過下列事情？

> 原文：With respect to the PISA test in your school, have you done any of the following?

## 選項量尺

是（Yes） ｜ 否（No） ｜ valid skip (filter rule)（valid skip (filter rule)） ｜ system missing（system missing） ｜ not administered/not applicable（not administered/not applicable） ｜ invalid（invalid） ｜ no response（no response）

## 題目

**1. 我和本校教職員分享 PISA 測驗實施的資訊**

　　原文：I have shared information about the PISA test administration with the staff at my school.
　　代碼：`SC209Q01DA`

**2. 我和本校學生分享 PISA 測驗實施的資訊**

　　原文：I have shared information about the PISA test administration with the students at my school.
　　代碼：`SC209Q02DA`

**3. 我和本校學生的家長或監護人分享 PISA 測驗實施的資訊**

　　原文：I have shared information about the PISA test administration with the parents or guardians of students at my school.
　　代碼：`SC209Q03DA`

**4. 我與本校的教師談過 PISA 測驗的重要性**

　　原文：I have spoken to teachers at my school about the importance of the PISA test.
　　代碼：`SC209Q04JA`

**5. 我與家長或監護人談過 PISA 測驗的重要性**

　　原文：I have spoken to parents or guardians about the importance of the PISA test.
　　代碼：`SC209Q05JA`

**6. 我鼓勵本校的學生在 PISA 測驗中盡力而為**

　　原文：I have encouraged the students at my school to do their best during the PISA test.
　　代碼：`SC209Q06JA`
