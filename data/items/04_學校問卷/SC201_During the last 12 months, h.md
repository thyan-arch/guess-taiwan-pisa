# SC201　During the last 12 months, how often did

**題組代碼**：`SC201`　**題數**：7　**問卷**：學校問卷  
**Annex B 表**：—

## 題幹

> 在過去 12 個月期間，您或學校其他行政主管（主任、組長）參與下列

> 原文：During the last 12 months, how often did you or other members of the <school management team> engage in the following activities?

## 選項量尺

從不或幾乎從不（Never or almost never） ｜ 大約一年一到兩次（About once or twice a year） ｜ 大約一個月一到兩次（About once or twice a month） ｜ 大約一週一到兩次（About once or twice a week） ｜ 每天或幾乎每天（Every day or almost every day）

## 題目

**1. 根據對課堂教學的觀察，給予教師回饋**

　　原文：Providing feedback to teachers based on observations of instruction in the classroom
　　代碼：`SC201Q03JA`

**2. 採取行動支援教師之間的合作，以發展新的教學法**

　　原文：Taking actions to support co-operation among teachers to develop new teaching practices
　　代碼：`SC201Q04JA`

**3. 採取行動確保教師負起責任，改善自己的教學技能**

　　原文：Taking actions to ensure that teachers take responsibility for improving their teaching skills
　　代碼：`SC201Q05JA`

**4. 採取行動確保教師感到要為學生的學習成果負責**

　　原文：Taking actions to ensure that teachers feel responsible for their students' learning outcomes
　　代碼：`SC201Q06JA`

**5. 向家長或監護人提供有關學校和學生表現的資訊**

　　原文：Providing parents or guardians with information on the school and student performance
　　代碼：`SC201Q07JA`

**6. 制定本校的專業發展計劃**

　　原文：Working on a professional development plan for this school
　　代碼：`SC201Q11JA`

**7. 與其他學校校長合作解決困難的工作任務**

　　原文：Collaborating with principals from other schools on difficult work tasks
　　代碼：`SC201Q12DA`
