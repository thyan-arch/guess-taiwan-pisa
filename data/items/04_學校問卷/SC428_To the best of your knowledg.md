# SC428　To the best of your knowledge, how often

**題組代碼**：`SC428`　**題數**：3　**問卷**：學校問卷  
**Annex B 表**：—

## 題幹



> To the best of your knowledge, how often have the majority of school staff done each of the following during <the last academic year>?

## 選項量尺

從不或幾乎從不（Never or almost never） ｜ 大約一年一到兩次（About once or twice a year） ｜ 大約一個月一到兩次（About once or twice a month） ｜ 大約一週一到兩次（About once or twice a week） ｜ 每天或幾乎每天（Every day or almost every day）
