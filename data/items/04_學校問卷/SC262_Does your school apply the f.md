## 題目

**1. Each student is provided with a digital device to use at school.**

　　代碼：`SC262Q01DA`

**2. Students have to share a digital device in the classroom because there are not enough devices for each student.**

　　代碼：`SC262Q02DA`

**3. Digital devices are used for extracurricular activities at school.**

　　代碼：`SC262Q03DA`

**4. The use of digital devices is restricted to the computer laboratories.**

　　代碼：`SC262Q04DA`

**5. Students can bring the school’s digital devices home for educational purposes.**

　　代碼：`SC262Q05DA`

**6. Students bring their own digital devices for learning at school.**

　　代碼：`SC262Q06DA`
