## 題目

**1. 你為做好 PISA 測驗付出了多少努力？**

　　原文：How much effort did you put into doing well on the PISA test?
　　代碼：`ST331Q01JA`

**2. 如果 PISA 測驗的結果會計入你的學校成績，你會付出多少努力？**

　　原文：How much effort would you have invested if your results from the PISA test were going to be counted in your <school marks>?
　　代碼：`ST331Q02JA`

**3. 現在思考你回答的PISA 問卷，你在提供準確答案方面付出了多少努力？**

　　原文：Now think about the PISA questionnaire you just answered. How much effort did you put into giving accurate answers?
　　代碼：`ST331Q03JA`
