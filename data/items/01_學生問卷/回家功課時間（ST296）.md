## 題幹

> 在平常要上學的一週中，你做以下科目家庭作業

> 原文：In a typical school week, approximately how much time do you spend on homework in the following subjects?

## 選項量尺

每天 30 分鐘以內（Up to 30 minutes a day） ｜ 每天超過 30 分鐘、1 小時以內（More than 30 minutes and up to 1 hour a day） ｜ 每天超過 1 小時、2 小時以內（More than 1 hour and up to 2 hours a day） ｜ More than 2 hour and up to 3 hours a day（More than 2 hour and up to 3 hours a day） ｜ More than 3 hour and up to 4 hours a day（More than 3 hour and up to 4 hours a day） ｜ 每天超過 4 小時（More than 4 hours a day）
